Animes

Anime is a type of Japanese animation that has become popular all around the world. Anime includes many different genres such as action, adventure, romance, comedy, fantasy, and science fiction. Some popular anime are Naruto, Attack on Titan, One Piece, and Demon Slayer. Anime is not only for children; many series explore friendship, emotions, challenges, and personal growth.
